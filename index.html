HTML→JS

HTML-to-JS Converter

Upload a ZIP of a static website. Every page becomes a JavaScript file, and only index.html and render.html stay as HTML. It all runs in your browser, so your files are never uploaded anywhere.

    This restructures delivery. It does not protect your code. Visitors can still read every page in their browser's developer tools. View-source will show an empty shell, so search engines and visitors without JavaScript may not see your content.
